Section 5

Order lifecycle — 13 states

Strict state machine. Each transition logged in order_status_history.

1 Creation & Payment

PAYMENT_PENDING→PENDING→CONFIRMED

Fail: PAYMENT_FAILED

2 Fulfillment

CONFIRMED→PACKING→READY_TO_SHIP→SHIPPED

AWB generated via Shiprocket/Delhivery

3 Delivery

SHIPPED→OUT_FOR_DELIVERY→DELIVERED

WhatsApp/SMS notification sent

4 Termination

CANCELLED→RETURNED→REFUNDED

Cancellation restocks inventory
